Smart Travel Tips for You

📅

Book in Advance

Save up to 40% by booking flights and hotels at least 6–8 weeks ahead. Early birds get the best deals!

🎒

Pack Light

Master the art of carry-on only travel. One bag means no checked baggage fees and faster airport exits.

💳

Use Travel Cards

Get a travel credit card with no foreign transaction fees. Earn miles on every purchase automatically.

🌍

Travel Insurance

Always insure your trips. It's a small cost for peace of mind against cancellations, medical needs, or lost bags.

🍜

Eat Like a Local

Skip tourist restaurants. Head to local markets and street food stalls for authentic flavors at half the price.

📱

Offline Maps

Download Google Maps offline for your destination. Never get lost even without mobile data abroad.
